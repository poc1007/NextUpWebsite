NextUp Privacy Policy

Your viewing data is yours.

Last updated 13 August 2026.

Who operates NextUp

NextUp is an independently operated TV-series companion app. This policy explains how the app handles the information needed to provide your account, viewing tools and recommendations.

What NextUp stores

NextUp stores your account identifier, username, country or region preference, profile visibility choice and onboarding preferences. It also stores the series you save, watch or complete; episode progress and watch dates; ratings; favourites; review ratings and any review text or tags you choose to save; and rewatch history and reflections.

How your information is used

NextUp uses this information to restore your account across devices, maintain your watch history and statistics, generate recommendations, personalise your Viewing Cloud and show profile information according to the visibility setting you choose.

Supabase and TMDB

NextUp uses Supabase for authentication and cloud data storage. NextUp requests TV-series metadata, images, genres, keywords, reviews and availability information from TMDB. TMDB handles those requests under its own privacy policy.

Profile visibility

You can choose whether your profile is private, visible to friends or public. Public-profile features display only the information permitted by your chosen visibility settings. Private written review text and tags remain private when you choose a private review visibility; the related show, season and star rating can still appear where NextUp presents public rating summaries.

Signing out and deletion

Signing out removes the account from the device while leaving your cloud account available for a future sign-in. Deleting your account permanently removes the associated cloud account and its stored data, subject to short operational backups that are deleted on their normal schedule.

Retention and changes

NextUp keeps your account data while your account remains active. We update this policy when app behaviour changes in a way that affects what NextUp collects, stores, shares or displays. The latest version is always published here.

Questions or requests

Use the Support page to ask about your information or request help. Do not send passwords, verification codes, password-reset links, API keys or access tokens.
